About Us

A home away from home in Oyarifa

Tucked in a quiet corner of Oyarifa, Accra, Woodcote Guest House was built around one simple idea: every guest should feel cared for, not just accommodated. Whether you're in town for business, a family visit, or a weekend reset, our rooms, meals and spaces are designed for genuine comfort.

We pair a calm, serene setting with attentive, friendly staff and the practical reliability travellers actually need — steady power, secure grounds, and service that doesn't stop at 5pm.
